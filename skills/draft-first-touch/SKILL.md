# Draft first touch

Follow `docs/skill-frame.md`.

## Use when

- A buyer has a verified email or a confirmed profile and is otherwise held only for review.
- You are writing the first touch.

## Steps

1. Read `knowledge.context.resolve`, `motion.local/offer.md`, and `brand/taste.md` (`references/evidence.md`).
2. Pass `docs/ai-tells.md` and keep the assigned line exact (`references/voice.md`).
3. Preview with `sequence.outreach.message_preview` and leave the draft unreviewed (`references/preview.md`).

## Stop

- dry_run first, quote, wait for the owner's yes on that item
- Do not send, enroll, launch, post, schedule, or delete.
