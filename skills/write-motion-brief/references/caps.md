# Caps

The audience cap is the maximum companies in one batch. The credit ceiling is the maximum credits that batch may quote. Both lines must have a number the owner stated.

There is no Dreamstate call in this check. Read the local file. A blank line, a question mark, or a word such as later counts as empty.

Show the owner the two numbers side by side. Ask them to confirm both before any source skill starts.

Stop and do not call build-account-list while either number is empty. Do not treat a missing ceiling as zero or as unlimited.
