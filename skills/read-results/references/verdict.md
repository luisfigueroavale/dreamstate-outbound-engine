# Verdict

The local read-results function groups by test, audience, variant, and prompt version. It counts a reply or a meeting only on a delivered message. Duplicate message ids collapse. A conflicting duplicate fails the read. The review floor is 100 delivered per variant.

The interval is a Wilson 95 percent interval. Ahead or behind only when the intervals do not overlap. Otherwise the words are no clear difference, or too little data under the floor.

Show the owner the verdict word and the interval, and say this is a reading, not a proof.

Stop if the read returns a conflicting duplicate. Do not average the two rows. Do not change weights in the same week without a learning note and a yes.
