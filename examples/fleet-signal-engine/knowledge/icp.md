# Ideal customer

Fictional example. No real companies or people.

Lanternway is a fictional maintenance scheduling tool for regional trucking and delivery fleets of 50 to 1500 vehicles in the US and Canada. Every domain ends in `.example`.

Include a fleet that runs its own shop, operates in that region, and is inside the size band.

Exclude brokers that do not run trucks, government yards, and fleets outside the region.

The real Ideal Customer page, when this is not an example, stays in Company Context.
