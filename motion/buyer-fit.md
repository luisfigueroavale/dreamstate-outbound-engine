# Buyer fit

Status: blank

Company fit and buyer fit are separate. A matching title does not prove the person can buy.

Walk roles in this order:

1. The economic buyer, the person who can say yes to the offer.
2. The champion, the person who feels the problem this week.
3. The user, the person who would live in the tool.

Skip an excluded profile. If the current employer or the current title is not confirmed, hold the row. Do not invent a profile URL.

| Check | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Title matches a role above | 20 |
| Remit is evidenced | 40 |
| Authority is evidenced | 40 |

Pass line: 60, and authority must be evidenced. Title alone never passes.
