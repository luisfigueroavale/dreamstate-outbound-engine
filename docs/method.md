# Method

Fit comes first. Signals raise a company that already fits. People and emails are bought only for tier A and tier B. Tier C stops. A draft is not a send.

The chain is: merge sources, research the company from cited pages, score company fit, score signals, rank accounts, find a buyer, check the role, score buyer fit, rank buyers, find and verify a work email, assign a test variant, draft one first touch, and check that the row is ready. Results come back as delivered, replies, and meetings. One learning note changes one variable.

Two paths can run in the same week. The coverage path touches every company that passed fit, slowly, under the cap. The signal path lets a fresh strong signal jump the queue. Fit still wins. A strong signal on a company that failed fit does not get a message.
